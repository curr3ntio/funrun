Studio notes · Issue 04

Everything worth building starts as a mess on the floor.

A short collection of thoughts on materials, patience and the strange joy of watching something slow happen. Scroll down, or just wait for the paint.

Viscosity

Thick paint moves like it has somewhere better to be but no particular hurry to get there. It bunches, hesitates, then lets go all at once.

Gloss

A wet surface tells you where the light is. Every bead carries a small, bent reflection of the room around it.

Gravity

Runs never fall in step. The wide ones lead, the thin ones lag, catch a neighbour and finally sprint the last stretch.

Settling

Even after the front has passed, nothing is truly still. Edges relax, tips sag, the film keeps breathing for a while.

The overlay above this page is a single WebGL2 triangle. There are no images, no DOM tricks and no CSS keyframes involved in the paint itself; every rivulet, bead and highlight is computed per pixel in a fragment shader from a handful of noise functions and one time value.
